# AI Quality Review Checklist

## Before reviewing

- [ ] Identify the user's goal.
- [ ] Extract explicit requirements and constraints.
- [ ] Note the expected format, tone, audience, and level of detail.
- [ ] Identify claims that need source verification.

## During review

- [ ] Check that every part of the task is addressed.
- [ ] Separate supported facts from assumptions.
- [ ] Look for hallucinations, contradictions, and invented details.
- [ ] Check calculations, units, definitions, and technical terminology.
- [ ] Confirm that reasoning is internally consistent.
- [ ] Remove irrelevant repetition or filler.
- [ ] Check that uncertainty is stated clearly.
- [ ] Review safety, privacy, and potential harm where relevant.

## Writing feedback

- [ ] Cite a specific issue rather than giving a vague judgment.
- [ ] Explain why the issue affects quality.
- [ ] Distinguish critical errors from minor improvements.
- [ ] Recommend a concise, actionable correction.
- [ ] Keep the final rationale consistent with the score.

## Final check

- [ ] Re-read the task.
- [ ] Confirm that the same standard was applied across examples.
- [ ] Ensure the review is clear, evidence-based, and respectful.
