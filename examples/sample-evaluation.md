# Sample Evaluation

## Task

Explain why location alone is not enough to determine soil-bearing capacity.

## Example response

"Two sites in the same city will always have the same soil-bearing capacity because they share the same climate and geology."

## Evaluation

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Instruction compliance | 4/5 | Addresses the topic but does not explain the required limitation fully. |
| Relevance | 5/5 | Directly discusses soil-bearing capacity. |
| Factual support | 1/5 | The central claim is unsupported and generally false. Soil properties can vary significantly over short distances. |
| Reasoning quality | 2/5 | It assumes shared climate and regional geology guarantee identical subsurface conditions. |
| Completeness | 2/5 | Omits site investigation, groundwater, soil type, density, stratification, and testing. |
| Clarity | 4/5 | The sentence is clear, but the conclusion is misleading. |
| Safety and uncertainty | 2/5 | It presents a high-confidence engineering conclusion without site-specific evidence. |

**Overall: 20/35 — Needs meaningful revision**

## Improved response

Location alone cannot determine soil-bearing capacity. Soil type, density, moisture, groundwater, fill, and subsurface layers can change significantly between nearby sites. A geotechnical investigation and site-specific testing are needed before selecting a design value.

## Reviewer rationale

The original response is concise but unreliable because it replaces site evidence with a broad geographic assumption. The revision states the limitation, identifies relevant variables, and recommends the correct verification step.
