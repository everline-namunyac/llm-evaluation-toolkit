# LLM Response Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | 1 — Poor | 3 — Acceptable | 5 — Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instruction compliance | Misses or violates key requirements | Meets most requirements with minor gaps | Fully follows all explicit and implicit constraints |
| Relevance | Mostly unrelated or padded | Generally relevant with some unnecessary content | Direct, focused, and useful |
| Factual support | Contains major unsupported or false claims | Mostly accurate but needs limited verification | Accurate, well-supported, and appropriately qualified |
| Reasoning quality | Contradictory, incomplete, or invalid | Understandable with minor gaps | Clear, consistent, and logically sound |
| Completeness | Omits critical information | Covers the main points | Fully addresses the task at the right depth |
| Clarity | Difficult to follow or ambiguous | Generally clear | Precise, well-organized, and easy to understand |
| Safety and uncertainty | Hides uncertainty or gives unsafe guidance | Notes some limits | Handles uncertainty, risk, and limitations responsibly |

## Overall rating

- 29–35: Strong
- 22–28: Good with minor revisions
- 15–21: Needs meaningful revision
- 7–14: Unreliable

## Reviewer notes

For every score below 5, record:

- the exact issue
- evidence from the response
- why it matters
- a practical correction
